Analysis of Customer Needs

During the dot-com bubble, many companies believed that "if you build it, they will come." Today, your marketing department should be asking, "if you build it, will they care?" and your CTO should be asking, "if we promise it, can we deliver it on time?" Degel can help your marketing team mesh customer expectations with the limitations of the technically feasible. We help you maximize the value of your R&D effort and get your development team in sync with your marketing requirements.

Protocol Design

Today's mobile applications require network connectivity. Degel has been designing network protocols for years and can create efficient protocols to meet your needs: UDP, TCP, HTTP, or WAP bearer; client-server, server-proxied, or peer-to-peer topology; Java or C++.

GUI Design

Mobile GUI design is challenging. Screen sizes are small, standard controls are limited, keypads are non-standard, and each manufacturer has different design rules. Whether your goal is to match the design of the target platform, emulate another device, or create your own branded look and feel, we will design the GUI you need.
