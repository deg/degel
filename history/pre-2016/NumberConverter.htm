Number Converter Utility

Degel Software is pleased to offer a free utility to our clients and friends.

We developed this program, NumberConverter.exe as an easy-to-use Microsoft Outlook utility. It will automatically update all your Israeli cellular phones numbers stored in Outlook, according to the changes implemented in April 2004.

This utility also standardizes the presentation of all Israeli and USA numbers. No other countries are directly supported, but the program does include an optional experimental option for numbers from other countries.
