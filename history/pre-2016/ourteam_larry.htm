Development Manager

Larry Rublin

Larry joined our staff in February 2004 with a broad IT background including system development, product management and system administration in a variety of technical and functional environments. His responsibilities at Degel include project management, business development and office administration. Most recently he served as the Project Manager for the development of Talk Now for Series 60 on behalf of Orange's Communications Products Organization.

Larry joined us with nearly two decades of prior experience:

  • At Amdocs, he served as a Project Manager in the R & D Generic Enhancements Group. The group identified, developed and marketed generic enhancements for Ensemble, Amdocs's flagship suite of Customer Care and Billing products. While at Amdocs, he also represented the company in the GSM Association's TADIG group, and served as the Product Manager for Ensemble's solutions for the billing of international roaming calls for GSM networks (TAP3 and RAP).
  • While a Project Manager at Malam Systems he managed the PC and distributed systems track of the Israel Police's Y2K project.
  • At IBM Global Services (formerly Tadiran Information Systems) he managed and/or marketed a variety of smart card related projects.
  • As IS Manager for Somekh Chaikin (KPMG Israel - formerly Price Waterhouse Israel) he managed the firm's internal computers, networks and applications for over 400 users, and represented the Israel affiliate in the various technical forums of the worldwide firm. While at Somekh Chaikin, he successfully implemented one of the country's first Lotus Notes systems and integrated it as a part of Price Waterhouse's worldwide Notes network. He also served as a Senior Consultant for the firm's consulting subsidiary and participated in a variety of development and EDP auditing projects for clients such as Intel, The Israel Electric Company and Bank Leumi.
  • At Andersen Consulting (now Accenture) he participated in a number of main frame development projects for financial and commercial clients.

Larry graduated from Northeastern Illinois University with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science in 1983 and earned a Master's in Business Administration (MBA) from the University of Chicago's Graduate School of Business in 1987.
