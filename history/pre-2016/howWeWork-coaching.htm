Coaching and training

Some of our clients like to do all of their core development in-house, but appreciate some assistance assimilating new technologies. For these clients, we offer coaching and training services to:

  • Find undocumented and under-documented platform APIs
  • demonstrate and prototype correct API usage
  • present effective platform tools and debugging techniques
  • set-up efficient mobile build environments
  • assist in debugging tough problems; and
  • enhance your productivity on each mobile platform.

Training on Mobile Development Tools

Mobile development is very different from PC or Unix/Linux development. Each mobile device has its own toolset and development chain. Skills acquired in desktop development, or even on other mobile devices, do not transfer easily to each new device, handset, or emulator environment.

We can teach your development staff how to develop and maintain mobile applications. Whether you need to learn about Symbian APIs, J2ME quirks, platform dependencies, development hacks, or any other facet of mobile software development, we can bring you up-to-speed, quickly and painlessly.

Authoring of White Papers, Tech Reports, and Presentations

Sometimes you need not only impromptu training for your staff, but also more formal documentation, whether for in-house use or for presentation to customers, investors, or partners.
