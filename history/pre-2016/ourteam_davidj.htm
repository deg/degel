Symbian Guru

David Jacobson

David joined Degel in 2001. He is Degel's resident Symbian Guru and all-around brainstorming genius. He often finds great solutions to clients' "impossible" problems. Though much of David's work remains confidential, we can boast that he succeeded in writing an after-market mouse driver for the Nokia 9210 Communicator, even though that device is configured not to allow the addition of any device drivers. David has most recently served as the lead developer for the Symbian version of VKB's virtual keyboard.

His past experience includes:

  • A member of the 2AM Technical Development team. While there, he helped architect the next generation of 2AM's Creation software, and was responsible for memory management, diagnostic and bug-catching tools.
  • Worked for eight years at Ligature/Wizcom as a programmer and algorithm designer with broad responsibility, from writing device drivers to improving the stability of the graphics detection algorithms to optimizing program performance.

David graduated England's Cambridge University with a Bachelor's in Mathematics in 1989 and earned a Master's degree in Mathematics from Imperial College in London, England in 1991.
