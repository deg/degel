Reading this honestly

Occupied time counts minutes where the sensor reported motion: active at the moment it was polled. It is not a headcount — one person and six people look identical.

Detected arrivals counts transitions from inactive to active. At a one-minute poll rate this undercounts badly: anyone who walks through and leaves between two polls is invisible, and a sensor that stays active across several polls registers one arrival, not several. Treat it as a floor, not a count.

Coverage. 43,029 of 43,107 possible minutes were sampled — 99.8%, with a single nine-minute gap on 12 August. The collection itself is reliable; the resolution is the limit.
