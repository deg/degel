Track record

A flag on every wave

דגל (degel) is Hebrew for “flag.” I’ve planted one on every technology wave since 1980 — not watching from shore, but shipping commercial software inside each shift.

1980–1990

TCP/IP, Lisp machines & the first AI wave

At MIT in 1980, I worked with the Laboratory for Computer Science team implementing the Internet’s TCP/IP protocols. Then straight into the first AI wave: at Symbolics — maker of the Lisp machine, and holder of the internet’s first registered domain (symbolics.com, 1985) — I built the Command Processor and major parts of the window system, then co-founded International Lisp Associates. After moving to Israel: image-processing and object-recognition toolkits on Lisp machines for MBT–Israel Aircraft Industries.

Era artifacts
I still keep a Lisp machine emulator running. Some loves last.
1990–1996

Windows, machine vision & translation

Major portions of a paint and drawing program for NTT, Japan’s telecom giant. Mission-critical components and advanced C++ techniques for Cognex machine vision. Ported a commercial machine-translation system to English and Japanese Windows. Medical software later acquired by McKesson. Much of the language-technology work ran through Basis Technology — a client relationship that continues to this day.

Era artifacts
1996–2001

The first internet boom

Co-founded 2AM Inc. as VP Technology and directed its core Creation engine — the scripting and runtime environment for a massively distributed, peer-to-peer game platform that served over 250,000 users in the dial-up era.

2001–2010

Mobile, before the iPhone

Ported Sun’s J2ME runtime to the first Symbian smartphones. Built ICQ’s and AOL’s mobile messaging clients. Push-to-talk for Orange UK. Shipped dozens of Symbian and J2ME apps — including a Nokia 9210 device driver that Nokia said couldn’t be written. Symbian named Degel a Platinum Partner, and Degel-built apps swept the Handango Champion awards. Co-founded Vringo as CTO, steered its mobile products through the arrival of the modern smartphone, and guided it to a NYSE IPO.

Era artifacts
2010–2022

The modern web & NLP

Full-stack product work for startups and established clients — including a Clojure-based biological-computation system for the National University of Singapore. As Principal Software Engineer at Basis Technology, I designed and built the Adaptation Studio and Identity Studio NLP front ends — and spearheaded the company’s early adoption of ChatGPT and LLM techniques, my on-ramp to the next wave.

Era artifacts
2023–now

AI-augmented development

The biggest shift I’ve seen in four decades, and I work inside it daily: LLM data pipelines, AI-accelerated MVPs, agentic coding workflows, AI-assisted patent drafting. Not a tourist — a resident. One recent weekend, working alongside Claude, I built a 13,000-line Scheme interpreter in Rust — a language I’d never used — passing all 1,225 tests of the standard conformance suite.

About

Degel Software is me — and, when a project needs it, the like-minded senior developers I’ve worked with for years.
