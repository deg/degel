Track record

A flag on every wave

דגל (degel) is Hebrew for “flag.” Since 1988 I’ve planted one on each technology wave as it arrived — not watching from shore, but shipping commercial software inside every shift.

1984–1990

Lisp machines & symbolic AI

Fresh from MIT and newly moved to Israel, I designed and built image-processing research toolkits on Symbolics Lisp machines — working in AI decades before it was fashionable.

I still keep a Lisp machine emulator running. Some loves last.
1990–1996

Windows, machine vision & translation

Major portions of a paint and drawing program for NTT, Japan’s telecom giant. Mission-critical components and advanced C++ techniques for Cognex machine vision. Ported a commercial machine-translation system to English and Japanese Windows. Medical software later acquired by McKesson.

Era artifacts
1996–2001

The first internet boom

Co-founded 2AM Inc. as VP Technology and directed its core Creation engine — the scripting and runtime environment for a massively distributed, peer-to-peer game platform that served over 250,000 users in the dial-up era.

2001–2010

Mobile, before the iPhone

Ported Sun’s J2ME runtime to the first Symbian smartphones. Built ICQ’s and AOL’s mobile messaging clients. Push-to-talk for Orange UK. Shipped dozens of Symbian and J2ME apps — including a Nokia 9210 device driver that Nokia said couldn’t be written. Co-founded Vringo as CTO, steered its mobile products through the arrival of the modern smartphone, and guided it to a NYSE IPO.

Era artifacts
2010–2022

The modern web & NLP

Full-stack product work for startups and established clients: biotech, financial systems, single-page applications. As Principal Software Engineer at Basis Technology, I designed and built the Adaptation Studio and Identity Studio NLP front ends — and spearheaded the company’s early adoption of ChatGPT and LLM techniques, my on-ramp to the next wave.

2023–now

AI-augmented development

The biggest shift I’ve seen in four decades, and I work inside it daily: LLM data pipelines, AI-accelerated MVPs, agentic coding workflows, AI-assisted patent drafting. Not a tourist — a resident.

About

David Goldfarb

Degel Software is me — and, when a project needs it, the like-minded senior developers I’ve worked with for years. I’ve kept the company deliberately small since 1988 so that everything it ships meets one quality standard: mine. If you ask me to build something that seems wrong, I’ll tell you before I build it. My goal is your project’s success, not billable hours. Clients across the decades have ranged from AOL, Sun, and Intel to Israel Aircraft Industries and the National University of Singapore.
