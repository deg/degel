Toys

Things I build for fun

I build these for fun, with AI agents writing most of the code and me doing the engineering: the design, the tests the code has to pass, review of everything that comes back, and refusing “done” until it is. I call it vibe engineering: the speed of vibe coding, with the discipline that makes the result trustworthy. My essays describe how that works in practice, and beads-utils is the tooling I built for it.

Cellular automata & more · Rust

Simple rules, strange worlds

Grids of cells that follow their neighbours, and a number squared over and over. Conway’s Game of Life, Langton’s ant, Wireworld, Lenia, snowflakes, sandpiles and the Mandelbrot set, all running live. Draw on the grid, change the rules, and watch what grows.

Z80 & CP/M 2.2 · Rust

CPeMulator

CP/M was the operating system most 8-bit business computers ran around 1980. This boots the genuine, unmodified CP/M 2.2 on a Z80 emulator written from scratch in Rust. Play Zork, write in WordStar, compile with Turbo Pascal, all at an A> prompt.

R7RS Scheme · Rust

nscheme

A Scheme interpreter in Rust: the R7RS-small core plus the R7RS-large libraries, compiled to WebAssembly so it runs in the page. Type into a live session, where your definitions build up as you go. It began as a weekend experiment in AI-assisted coding.
